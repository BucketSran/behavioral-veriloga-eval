## Brief and claim

Prepare the first small, reproducible **workflow comparison** between legacy
mini-swe and `native-mini-swe`, using unchanged r53 and EVAS 0.8.7. This is not
the old `--agent-scaffold native` sensitivity loop, an architecture-only causal
ablation, a reproduction of paper scores, or permission to start a paid run.
Keep Reasoning, Evolution, RAG, waveform and training out of this baseline.

Acceptance: a reviewable protocol and execution tracker name the exact tasks,
conditions, controls, allowed differences, run order, costs/stop rules and
claim boundaries; free checks inspect actual request/public-input surfaces;
every unresolved launch prerequisite is explicit. A passing offline check is
not model performance or authorization to contact a provider. No inherited
budget: model/service identity and a fresh fee cap remain launch blockers.
